About Jane

Jane Doe writes novels of coast and weather. She grew up two streets from a working harbour, spent ten years as a ferry dispatcher, and now writes from a house where the tide tables are pinned beside the kettle.

The Salt Road, her first novel, was shortlisted for the Harbourlight Prize. She is at work on a fourth book, set one generation earlier.
